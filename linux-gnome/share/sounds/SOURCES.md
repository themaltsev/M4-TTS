# Bundled sound sources

- `speech-start.oga` is the unmodified Yaru `message-new-instant.oga` cue.
  Copyright Mads Rosendahl; CC BY-SA 4.0. Source package: Ubuntu `yaru-theme-sound`,
  `/usr/share/sounds/Yaru/stereo/message-new-instant.oga`.
- `speech-cancel.oga` is the unmodified freedesktop `dialog-information.oga` cue.
  Copyright Ivica Bukvic; CC BY-SA 3.0. Source package: `sound-theme-freedesktop`,
  `/usr/share/sounds/freedesktop/stereo/dialog-information.oga`.
- `openclaw-soft-tick.wav` is the project's soft waiting tick.
